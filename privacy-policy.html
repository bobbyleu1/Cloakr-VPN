Privacy Policy for Cloakr VPN

Last updated: January 2025

1. Introduction

Vroom Automotive Group ("we," "our," or "us") operates the Cloakr VPN mobile application (the "Service"). This Privacy Policy informs you of our policies regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of personal data when you use our Service.

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Information You Provide

  • Purchase information for subscription management
  • App usage analytics (aggregated and anonymized)

2.2 Information We Don't Collect

Cloakr does not log, store, or inspect the contents of your internet traffic. We do not collect:

  • Websites you visit
  • Files you download
  • DNS queries
  • IP addresses you connect to
  • Connection timestamps
  • Bandwidth usage details

3. VPN Service Disclosure

Cloakr routes your internet traffic through our VPN servers to provide a secure connection. We do not inspect, log, or store the contents of your traffic. Our VPN service establishes an encrypted tunnel between your device and our servers solely for the purpose of providing secure internet access.

4. Third-Party Services

4.1 Google AdMob

Our app displays advertisements through Google AdMob. AdMob may collect and use advertising identifiers and other data for ad personalization. You can opt out of personalized advertising in your device settings.

4.2 Google Play Billing

Subscription purchases are processed through Google Play Billing. Google's privacy policy governs the collection and use of payment information.

5. Data Usage

We use collected information to:

  • Provide and maintain our VPN service
  • Process subscription payments
  • Improve app performance and user experience
  • Display relevant advertisements (if you're using the free tier)

6. Data Sharing

We do not sell or rent your personal information to third parties. We may share information only in the following circumstances:

  • With service providers who help us operate our service (under strict data protection agreements)
  • When required by law or to protect our rights
  • In connection with a business transfer or acquisition

7. Data Security

We implement industry-standard security measures to protect your data. All VPN connections use strong encryption protocols. However, no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure.

8. Data Retention

We retain personal information only as long as necessary to provide our services or as required by law. Connection logs and usage statistics are automatically deleted after 30 days.

9. Children's Privacy

Our Service is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you become aware that a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us.

10. International Data Transfers

Your information may be transferred to and processed in countries other than your own. We ensure appropriate safeguards are in place for such transfers.

11. Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights regarding your personal information, including:

  • Right to access your data
  • Right to correct inaccurate data
  • Right to delete your data
  • Right to data portability
